AIL v7.0 introduces a new feature to collect, analyse, extract and correlate forums.

<img width="1832" height="1233" alt="Screenshot from 2026-07-09 16-29-22" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8cae4a7a-d235-4d7f-8d73-a496cd637298" />

Forums are useful sources of information, but they are often difficult to monitor. Many forums require an account, sessions must be maintained over time, and collectors may face captchas, scraping protection, bans, or other access restrictions. Useful information is also spread across subforums, threads, posts, users, images, reactions, and quoted messages.

With this release, AIL can keep the forum structure and make it easier to analyse. Instead of storing forum pages as simple web captures, AIL now understands forums as dedicated objects:

* Forums
* Subforums
* Threads
* Posts
* User accounts

This makes forum data easier to browse, search, translate, track, correlate, and use in investigations.

## Main features

### Forum browsing

* Browse forums, subforums, threads, and posts in a dedicated interface.
* Keep the structure of the forum, including breadcrumbs and parent-child relationships.
* View related user accounts, images, reactions, and quoted content.
* Sort threads by last activity.

### Forum extraction

* Extract structured data from forum HTML pages.
* Import forums, subforums, threads, posts, users, reactions, images, URLs into AIL.
* Detect languages and translate forum posts.
* Handle post images and user images for analysis and correlation.

### Authenticated forum crawling

* Manage forum crawler accounts.
* Support authenticated collection using cookies and local storage.
* Schedule forum, subforum, and thread crawl tasks.
* View crawler status, pending tasks, running tasks, errors, and queues.

### Search, tracking, and correlation

* Search forum posts and forum threads.
* Filter search results by forum.
* Use trackers to monitor forum content.
* Correlate forums, subforums, threads, posts, users, images, usernames, and other AIL objects.
* Track usernames across different forums to identify reuse, links, and recurring activity.

### Analyst workflows

* View forum user accounts and their activity.
* Browse posts from a specific forum user.
* View activity statistics for forum users.
* Search for specific information inside collected forum content.
* Pivot from a post to its author, thread, subforum, images, reactions, and related objects.
